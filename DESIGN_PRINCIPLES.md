# Design principles

## Gameplay
- When deciding on gameplay mechanics and how they work, always favor letting the player cheat a little bit over perfect fairness. It is better for a player to be surprised that something worked when they thought it shouldn't, over the player getting frustrated because something did not work they they thought it should.
- Gameplay elements should ideally be designed around the wingmen system.

## HUD
- The player's attention is limited, and so we should reduce the amount of text they have to read. Favor visual indicators over plain text. Favor simplicity over complexity.
- Whenever possible, static visual elements (elements that are not updated) should be rendered once and reused each frame.

## Visual style

- Cel-shaded, animated look.
- Favor effects that look like 2D animation.
- Favor the use of shaders.

## Character dialogue and personalities

The Star Fox team's relationship with one another has evolved into family territory over the years. They're not above conflict or disagreement, but at the end of the day they respect each other deeply and would (and do) trust each other with their lives.

### Fox

Serious, professional, strong sense of justice, but also more relaxed than he used to be in Assault.

#### Dialogue

Direct and composed. Confident without being arrogant. Doesn't need to constantly remind everyone that he's the leader.

#### Relationships

**Falco:** Playful and brotherly, rooted in deep respect after fighting alongside each other for so many years. Even though they might disagree and bicker, behind the cockpit Fox would trust Falco with his life a thousand times.

**Slippy:** Playful and brotherly. He likes to bust Slippy's balls from time to time. All in good fun, however. Has deep respect for Slippy's intellect.

**Krystal:** Openly romantic, they do not hide it. Rooted in deep trust and affection. Playful and relaxed. His dialogue towards Krystal can be cute and/or a little bit romantic if fitting, though he tends to keep it professional on the battlefield.

### Krystal

Cheerful, but not comedic. Feminine, but not a bimbo. Fun and easygoing, but also highly focused and professional when the situation calls for it.

#### Dialogue

Warm and expressive, but capable of being firm and concise. Avoid making her excessively bubbly or turning her into the team's designated source of emotional support.

#### Relationships

**Fox:** Openly romantic, they do not hide it. Rooted in deep trust and affection. Her dialogue towards Fox can be cute and/or a little bit romantic if fitting.

**Slippy:** Playful and sisterly. She tends to give Slippy more of a break than Fox or Falco, but she's not above cracking a joke at his expense from time to time. Like everyone else, she deeply respects his intellect.

**Falco:** Playful and sisterly. They share a penchant to bust each other's balls. They highly respect one another as both people and warriors.

### Falco

The team's ace pilot, Falco is brash and cocky, but he has undoubtedly matured greatly. He now accepts his place within the Star Fox hierarchy, and respects Fox's.

#### Dialogue

Sharp-tongued and cocky. Doesn't need to insult someone in every other sentence to sound like Falco. Avoid making him out to be an asshole.

#### Relationships

**Fox:** Playful and brotherly, rooted in deep respect after fighting alongside each other for so many years. Falco used to resent Fox being the leader of Star Fox, but after so many years and so many battles fought alongside each other, Falco has come to view Fox as a very capable pilot and leader, and nowadays Fox is the only person Falco would ever willingly take orders from. Even though they might disagree and bicker, behind the cockpit Falco would trust Fox with his life a thousand times.

**Krystal:** Playful and sisterly. They share a penchant to bust each other's balls. They highly respect one another as both people and warriors.

**Slippy:** Playful and brotherly. He likes to bust Slippy's balls from time to time, more than anyone else. All in good fun, however, and deep down he considers Slippy one of his closest friends. Has deep respect for Slippy's intellect.

### Slippy

Intelligent, enthusiastic, and good-natured. The team's technical genius, responsible for maintaining and upgrading their equipment. While he's not the greatest pilot in the galaxy, his intellect and ingenuity make him an invaluable member of the team. Can be excitable and occasionally insecure, but has proven himself time and time again.

#### Dialogue

Enthusiastic and technically minded. Can get excited when discussing engineering, but shouldn't constantly whine, panic, or explain things nobody asked about.

#### Relationships

**Fox:** Playful and brotherly. Slippy is comfortable enough around Fox to take his teasing in stride, and occasionally gives as good as he gets. Deeply respects Fox as both a friend and a leader, and trusts him with his life.

**Krystal:** Playful and friendly. Slippy appreciates Krystal's patience and easygoing nature. Deeply respects her as both a friend and a warrior.

**Falco:** Playful and brotherly. Slippy is the frequent target of Falco's teasing, but knows it's all in good fun. Occasionally gets frustrated with him, but considers him one of his closest friends and deeply respects his abilities as a pilot.

## General dialogue guidelines

### Team dynamics

- The team's banter should feel natural and affectionate, reflecting years of friendship and shared experiences.
- Teasing is fine, but it should never undermine the genuine respect the characters have for one another.
- Characters can disagree, argue, and get frustrated with each other. Their bond should remain apparent even during conflicts.
- Never make one character look stupid just to make another look cool. Each character has their own strengths and weaknesses, and the team works because those strengths complement one another.
- Avoid exaggerated personalities, forced jokes, and dialogue that exists solely to make a character look foolish.

### Combat and downtime

- Combat dialogue should prioritize useful information, tactical coordination, and concise reactions. Personality should emerge naturally rather than take precedence over the situation.
- During downtime, the team has more room for teasing, casual conversation, affection, and discussions unrelated to the mission.
- Characters should remain competent and professional during combat, even when under pressure. They can make mistakes, struggle, or get outmatched without becoming caricatures.

### Character-specific guidelines

- **Fox:** Don't make him infallible or excessively authoritative. He's a capable leader who trusts his team and knows when to listen to them.
- **Krystal:** Don't reduce her to a love interest or a source of exposition. She's an accomplished pilot, a capable warrior, and an independent member of the team.
- **Falco:** Don't portray him as a reckless idiot just to make Fox look like a better leader. He's an exceptionally skilled pilot who has matured and learned to respect his teammates.
- **Slippy:** Never portray him as incompetent for the sake of a joke. He can be excitable, insecure, or outmatched in a dogfight, but he's a brilliant engineer whose contributions are essential to the team's success. The others may tease him, but they deeply respect him and his abilities.

### Fox and Krystal

- Their relationship is established, openly romantic, and rooted in deep trust, respect, and affection.
- Their affection should feel natural and comfortable, not awkward or melodramatic.
- Romantic dialogue can be cute, playful, or subtly affectionate when appropriate.
- Their relationship shouldn't interfere with their professionalism during combat.
- NO SEXUAL INNUENDOS.
